About Sphere Studio

For capture, use a phone with a rear camera and motion sensors. HTTPS or localhost is required for browser camera access. iOS Safari may ask for motion permission after a tap.

Move slowly to align the aiming circle with each target, or use manual capture. Complete as many positions as possible before stitching. For best quality, avoid nearby objects or moving subjects.

What you can download: a standard 2:1 panoramic JPEG, a self-contained HTML viewer, a multi-scene HTML virtual tour, and a JSON tour manifest.

Limitation: Motion-guided spherical compositing is an approximate approach; it cannot fully remove parallax or exposure seams like professional computer-vision stitching. Processing may be intensive on older phones.
